# Salary evidence

Use live sources for every estimate. Record the source URL, publication or observation date, location, role/level, pay period, currency, contract type, and whether the figure is base cash or total compensation. A direct vacancy band applies only if it covers the candidate's residence and contract arrangement.

## Source order

1. **The employer's current vacancy or pay policy:** an applicable published base-pay band is the strongest evidence of the employer's likely budget. Check location, level, contract, and whether the band is base or total compensation.
2. **Recent comparable vacancies with disclosed pay:** prefer the same city or residence market, described duties, required skills and knowledge, role scope, seniority, contract type, and vacancy type. A matching title alone is weak evidence. Expand to metro or country only when needed, and label the fallback.
3. **Company and role pay reports:** [Levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/about/) and [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/blog/salary-estimates-in-jobs-what-employers-need-to-know/) can triangulate a range. Note report date, sample size if available, location, and whether the figure is submitted pay, verified pay, or an algorithmic estimate. Levels.fyi often presents total compensation; separate base pay before comparison. A Glassdoor estimate is not the employer's published band.
4. **Local occupation or job-board surveys:** use as broader anchors when direct comparisons are sparse. Examples include [US BLS occupational wage tables](https://www.bls.gov/oes/tables.htm), [Eurostat's Structure of Earnings Survey](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/microdata/structure-of-earnings-survey), [Poland's GUS occupation wage data](https://stat.gov.pl/en/topics/labour-market/working-employed-wages-and-salaries-cost-of-labour/structure-of-wages-and-salaries-by-occupations-for-october-2024%2C4%2C10.html), and [Just Join IT's salary report and methodology](https://justjoin.it/salary-report/methodology). Check release date and whether the figures are actual pay or advertised ranges.

Prefer evidence that matches the vacancy over a source's general reputation. Do not use a product-company or outsourcing multiplier without data for this market and role. Do not infer an employer budget from an unrelated country by currency conversion. If sources conflict, explain why the more comparable ones carry more weight.

## Asking range

Keep the raw market evidence distinct from the range recommended to say to HR. Where the employer publishes an applicable band, choose an upper but credible part **inside** that band; flag when the whole band trails comparable pay. Otherwise, use the upper part supported by multiple comparable observations. Avoid a spurious formula or narrow range when the evidence is sparse. With thin evidence, state a provisional range, low confidence, and which fact would improve it most.

For exchange-rate display, the [ECB daily reference rates](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/index.en.html) quote currencies against EUR and are generally updated on working days. They are reference rates for information, not transaction quotes. Show the rate date. Currency conversion changes the unit of the **same asking range**; it does not change the residence-market estimate.
