# Job Search Framework

The framework helps a job seeker assess vacancies and prepare compensation expectations for interviews.

## Language

**Asking range**:
A defensible fixed-cash compensation range the job seeker can state to a recruiter for a specific vacancy. It reflects comparable pay in the job seeker's residence market and the vacancy's circumstances; bonus and equity are separate.
_Avoid_: precise salary, guaranteed offer

**Residence market**:
The job seeker's location used to select local compensation comparisons, including for a remote vacancy.
_Avoid_: employer headquarters market

**Role scope**:
The duties, responsibilities, required skills, and required knowledge of a vacancy that determine the level of work expected. The vacancy title is only a label and may not reflect this scope.
_Avoid_: role title

**Vacancy type**:
The work arrangement for a specific vacancy: Product means building the hiring company's own product; Outsource means delivering a client project through a provider; Outstaff means working within a client team while supplied by a provider. A company can have vacancies of different types.
_Avoid_: company model, contract type

**Contract type**:
The legal and commercial arrangement under which the job seeker would be paid, such as employment or contractor/B2B. It is distinct from the vacancy type.
_Avoid_: vacancy type
